# Review authenticity

**Status:** Policy template. Enforcement of disclosures and purchase verification is **Deferred** to Phase 2–3.

Reviews may be personal opinions. FindShare does not independently verify that a reviewer purchased or used a product unless a future verification program is implemented and documented as such.

Planned disclosure types: purchased by reviewer, gifted, sponsored, affiliate, brand relationship, free sample, unverified purchase.

Do not create fake engagement counts. Seed data must be labeled demonstration data.

This policy requires qualified legal review before public release.
