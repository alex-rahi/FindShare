# Monetization roadmap

**Status:** Roadmap only. No revenue has been collected. Placeholder ads are **Deferred** to Phase 5 and must be labeled Advertisement/Sponsored/Affiliate Link. Live advertising is disabled by default.

Extension points (not implemented):

- Display advertising (`AdProvider` / `MockAdProvider`)
- Sponsored brands and sponsored products (never altering organic ratings)
- Affiliate product links (adapter interface only)
- Premium or ad-free memberships
- Authorized retailer integrations

Never disguise ads as reviews. Never let payment change ordinary user-review ratings. Never represent placeholder `MockAdEvent` rows as revenue. Do not include real ad SDKs or third-party tracking in the first release. Do not process purchases in the first release.

Legal templates require qualified legal review before public release.
